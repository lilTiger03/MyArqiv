# myArqiv 🎬

**Your films. Your series. Your personal collection.**

myArqiv is a mobile app in development for discovering, tracking, and discussing films and television series from around the world.

The project aims to make international discovery a central part of the experience, including Tamil cinema, South Korean films and series, Hollywood, and other film industries.

## Planned Features

- **Personal collection:** Track films and series you have watched.
- **Watchlist:** Save titles you want to watch next.
- **Global discovery:** Search and explore titles across languages, countries, and genres.
- **Personalised recommendations:** Discover titles based on your interests, ratings, and viewing history.
- **Film and series details:** Explore summaries, release dates, genres, and cast information.
- **Actor profiles:** View photos, filmographies, and available social media links.
- **Ratings and reviews:** Share your opinions and read other users’ reviews.
- **Community discussions:** Comment on films and series and connect with other viewers.

These features are planned and are not all implemented yet.

## Technology

The planned technology stack includes:

- **React Native and Expo** for a shared iOS and Android app
- **TypeScript** for application development
- **TMDB API** for film, television, and cast metadata, subject to API access approval and applicable terms
- **A backend and database** for accounts, watchlists, ratings, reviews, and comments; technology selection is pending
- **Git and GitHub** for version control and project documentation

## Project Status

**Early development.** Initial work focuses on setting up the mobile app, connecting film data, and building the core search and collection experience.

The first milestone is to allow a user to search for a title, open its details, and add it to a watched list or watchlist.

## Purpose

myArqiv is an independent app project

The goal is to develop practical skills in mobile development, API integration, database design, user experience, and software engineering.

The app is intended to be free for users. At this stage, the project is non-commercial, with no advertising, subscriptions, or paid promotions.

## Data and Attribution

TMDB is the planned source of film, television, and cast information. Integration will follow TMDB’s licensing and attribution requirements.

This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

Film posters, actor images, and other third-party content remain subject to their respective rights and terms.
